## Why this family exists

Majestic represents the pinnacle of **high-end interior design and luxury furniture e-commerce**. Rather than relying on busy retail clutter, it creates a deeply serene, aspirational shopping experience through editorial italic typography (Newsreader), full-bleed cinematic room photography, and thoughtful interactive tools like tiered bundle builders and room transformation comparisons.

## When to reach for it

- Luxury furniture, modern lighting, and interior design boutiques
- High-end home goods, bespoke decor, and architectural hardware brands
- Editorial lifestyle and hospitality design storefronts
- Curated home catalogs that sell coordinated spaces rather than isolated commodities

## Key visual signatures

1. **Newsreader Italic Serifs**: Light italic serifs for all display headlines, creating an unmistakable high-fashion editorial presence.
2. **Warm Cream & Charcoal Contrast**: Alternating clean white surfaces with warm cream accents (`#FFF3E1`) and deep charcoal frames (`#111111`).
3. **Tiered Bundle Builder**: Visual discount ladder displaying instant percentage savings as multiple products are bundled.
4. **Full-Bleed Collection Backdrops**: Atmospheric room scenes acting as navigation hubs with linked italic categories.
5. **Room Transformation Compare**: Interactive before/after showcase demonstrating styling impact.
6. **Product-Linked Testimonial Spotlight**: Customer review card directly featuring the reviewed piece with thumbnail and title.
