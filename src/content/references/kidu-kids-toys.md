## Why this family exists

Kidu shows how **a modern children's and toy e-commerce brand can feel vibrant, joyful, and deeply trustworthy without falling into chaotic clutters**. By organizing the storefront around a multi-scheme color system (#CE126E fuchsia, #584592 plum, #99EBEB mint, #FDE9F3 blush, #FFF0D8 sand) and anchoring everything with clean rounded cards, friendly typography (Nunito), and clear visual categorization (shop by age, flash countdowns, color swatches), it delivers an engaging, high-conversion shopping experience.

## When to reach for it

- Kids, baby, toy, and family lifestyle e-commerce brands
- Plushie, character merchandise, stationery, and creative gift shops
- Playful D2C lifestyle products, cute apparel, and novelty accessories
- Multi-category e-commerce sites needing clear visual age/type segmentation

## Key visual signatures

1. **Multi-Scheme Color Tokens**: 6 distinct harmonious color schemes (Clean White, Off-white #F4F4F4, Fuchsia #CE126E, Blush #FDE9F3, Warm Sand #FFF0D8, Deep Plum #584592 + Mint #99EBEB).
2. **Rounded Friendly Typography**: Nunito 800/900 rounded sans-serif across all display titles and badges.
3. **Eclipse Oval Age Filters**: Distinct vertical oval pills for age-based browsing.
4. **Interactive Product Swatches**: Top-card floating color picker dots on product listings.
5. **Flash Sale Urgency Elements**: Embedded countdown timers with distinct days/hours/mins/secs blocks.
6. **Asymmetrical Promo Collages**: Mixed 2x3 and 2x2 photo collage grids with shine hover effects.
7. **Comprehensive Value Badges**: Trust badges for lifetime repairs, free shipping, and global delivery.
