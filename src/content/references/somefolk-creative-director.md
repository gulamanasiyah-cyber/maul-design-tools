## Why this family exists

Somefolk demonstrates how **an independent creative portfolio can break completely out of sterile minimalism without losing its high-fashion editorial sharpness**. By pairing intense, high-voltage acid lime typography (`#C5F82A`) on deep void forest black (`#0A0F0D`) with endearing 1930s rubber-hose cartoon illustrations and cinematic photography, it communicates unparalleled creative personality, bespoke craftsmanship, and anti-commodity human authenticity.

## When to reach for it

- Art directors, creative developers, design directors, and multidisciplinary artists
- Independent creative studios, branding boutiques, and bespoke digital agencies
- Fashion-forward web design, cultural editorial projects, and experimental portfolios
- Any brand or creator championing handcrafted human creativity over generic AI templates

## Key visual signatures

1. **High-Voltage Acid Lime & Void Forest**: Intense `#C5F82A` contrasting against `#0A0F0D`.
2. **Ultra-Tall Condensed Headlines**: Towering display typography with tight negative tracking.
3. **1930s Rubber-Hose Cartoon Mascots**: Character illustrations (boy with monstera, boy holding hands with CRT monitor).
4. **Stacked Colored Folder Project Tabs**: Multi-layer card deck navigation with rich wine maroon accents.
5. **Rotating Concentric Seal Badge**: Animated circular graphic stamping Webflow & bespoke production credentials.
6. **Solid Lime Contact Card & Tennis Group**: Punchy color-block form paired with vibrant lifestyle photography.
