## Why this family exists

"Be Yours" is a demonstration storefront for the premium **Be Yours** Shopify theme by RoarTheme, showing how a cosmeceutical brand can feel both clinical and warm. It strips e-commerce to near-white surfaces and a single acid-lime accent (#DFFF4F), lets a **Jost + Assistant** geometric pairing carry the editorial voice, and leans heavily on **interactive conversion devices** — a live countdown bar, a 4-question skin quiz that assembles a routine, a hoverable skin-tone fan, shop-the-look hotspots, and a draggable before/after result — instead of flashy layout tricks.

## When to reach for it

- Skincare, cosmeceutical, supplements, and clean-beauty brands
- Any product store selling consultation-driven, personalization-led purchases
- Modern retail that wants a "clinical-white + one electric accent" identity
- Stores that replace collections with guided routines, quizzes, or recommendation flows

## Key visual signatures

1. **Acid Lime Highlight**: One loud #DFFF4F accent for underlines, scribbles, boxed words, and dark-section buttons — used sparingly over white.
2. **Jost + Assistant**: Geometric Jost display headings paired with clean Assistant body and uppercase letter-spaced captions.
3. **Countdown Urgency**: Black announcement bars with live day/hour/min/second countdown chips.
4. **Guided Skin Quiz**: A 4-question, one-screen-at-a-time flow that resolves into a priced 3-step routine.
5. **Skin-Tone Color Fan**: Hover-expanding fan of tone swatches proving "one formula, every tone".
6. **Shop-the-Look Hotspots**: Numbered markers over editorial imagery that open quick-view product popovers.

## Anti-patterns (instantly break the vibe)

- More than one loud accent color — the brand survives on exactly one electric lime note
- Decorative or script display type — clean geometric sans only
- Busy backgrounds behind product — near-white and full-bleed photography only
- A static "collection grid" with no guidance — this family personalizes via quiz/routine devices
- Soft pastels or gradients — surfaces stay clinical white or deep ink
