## Why this family exists

Distilled 100% 1:1 from the iconic **Scalter Variable Font specimen poster by Dirtyline Studio**. It proves how high-energy retro typography (chunky 3D serifs, vibrant green script accents, cartoon pointing hands, and a sticker-bomb badge cluster) can make a font release feel iconic, collectible, and unforgettable.

## When to reach for it

- Typeface launches, font foundry showcases, and typography spec sheets
- Streetwear, skate, and vintage apparel branding drops
- Music festivals, vinyl record packaging, and pop-culture poster designs
- Playful creative agency portfolios and retro-maximalist landing pages

## Anti-patterns (instantly break the vibe)

- Muted or pastel colors — the contrast between Cobalt Blue #1B2D92 and Electric Orange #FF5500 is essential
- Thin delicate modern strokes — all hero elements require bold cartoon outlines and heavy weight
- Minimalist whitespace — the sticker section must feel packed, overlapping, and energetic
- Plain flat text for the title — "SCALTER" must have multi-layered 3D extrusion and shadows
