## Why this family exists

Blume proves that **nature and gardening products do not need to look like serious traditional nurseries or sterile agro-chemical brands**. By infusing the identity with joyful Gen-Z internet culture (friendly smileys, lowercase chunky typography, asterisk brand punctuation, cloud speech bubbles, and playful seed naming like *mood boost* and *good cookin'*), it turns urban gardening into a delightful, accessible daily habit.

## When to reach for it

- D2C seed kits, indoor plants, microgreen growers, and countertop herb gardens
- Youth-focused wellness, botanical teas, herbal remedies, and organic skincare
- Playful e-commerce brands targeting Gen Z / Millennial apartment dwellers
- Any lifestyle product looking to make nature approachable, tactile, and fun

## Key visual signatures

1. **Meadow Hero & Floating Nav Pills**: High-angle wild dandelion meadow with 3 minimalist floating pill links.
2. **Chunky Lowercase `blume*` Typography**: Rounded ultra-bold lowercase typography punctuated with an asterisk.
3. **Illustrated Smiley Pouches**: Cheerful floral character illustrations paired with clean packaging metadata and quantity steppers.
4. **Scalloped Cloud on Grass Blades**: Organic cloud speech bubble floating over hyper-textured green lawn photography.
5. **Flat-Lay Variety Box Showcase**: Dynamic isometric/flat-lay arrangement of box components, sachets, and foldout instructions.
