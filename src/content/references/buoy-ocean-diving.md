## Why this family exists

Buoy proves that **extreme sports and ocean expeditions can feel inviting, friendly, and deeply trusted rather than intimidating or clinical**. By balancing high-grade underwater photography (coral walls, shipwrecks, pelagic wildlife) with warm sand cream backgrounds (`#FBF7EC`), electric ocean blue (`#2B8BF5`), and endearing retro cartoon mascots, it turns scuba diving into an accessible lifestyle dream.

## When to reach for it

- Scuba diving clubs, liveaboard expeditions, and freediving schools
- Surfing camps, marine conservation foundations, and oceanic wildlife tours
- Coastal resorts, nautical gear brands, and yacht charter experiences
- Any adventure or outdoor brand that wants a sunny, retro-modern, coastal feel

## Key visual signatures

1. **Sand Cream & Ocean Azure Contrast**: Warm cream canvas (`#FBF7EC`) alternating with high-voltage ocean blue (`#2B8BF5`).
2. **Retro Scuba Cartoon Mascots**: Playful frogs and octopuses integrated directly into heavy display typography.
3. **Marine Line-Art & Seaweed Bands**: Delicate monoline kelp, coral, and jellyfish doodles framing major transitions.
4. **Solid Blue Number Blocks**: High-contrast blue stat boxes (`45+`, `12`, `8`) on cream surfaces.
5. **Underwater Photo Carousel**: Vertical framing of cenotes, sunken wrecks, and sea life encounters.
6. **Giant Cropped Bottom Wordmark**: Monumental lowercase `buoy` logo bleeding off the dark navy footer.
