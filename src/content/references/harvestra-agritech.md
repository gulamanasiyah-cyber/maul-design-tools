## Why this family exists

Harvestra proves how **heavy modern agricultural & climate tech can feel fresh, high-trust, and cutting-edge without falling into generic SaaS clichés**. By grounding the interface in visceral, high-resolution farming photography (aerial field geometry, dark rich soil, golden wheat harvesters, wrapped silage bales) and framing it with sharp pill UI components, sunny yellow accents (`#F4CD56`), and warm sand banner blocks (`#D9C7AE`), it bridges industrial credibility with venture-scale tech refinement.

## When to reach for it

- AgriTech, smart farming, precision agriculture, and autonomous farm machinery platforms
- Climate tech, carbon soil credits, regenerative agriculture, and irrigation intelligence tools
- B2B supply chain, commodity trading, agronomy consulting, and bio-tech agricultural ventures
- Any green/nature brand that needs industrial authority rather than delicate botanical styling

## Key visual signatures

1. **Circular Aerial Farmland Hero**: Full-bleed lush field geometry with floating pill navigation, white CTA pill, and golden-yellow play button.
2. **Asymmetric 3-Card Services Bento**: Deep crop photography paired with dark glassmorphic floating information panels.
3. **Numbered Dark Value Cards**: Bold `01 TM`, `02 TM`, `03 TM` serial numerals directly on soil and furrowed field textures.
4. **Sand & Golden Wheat Split Banner**: Full-bleed combine harvester photo paired with warm sand beige (`#D9C7AE`) and stacked display typography.
5. **Silage Bale & AI Detection Split**: Practical farm photography (white silage plastic bales) paired with clean AI feature breakdowns.
6. **Farmetic Marquee Footer**: Deep black-forest field with high-contrast oversized ticker typography.
