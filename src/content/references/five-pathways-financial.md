## Five Pathways Financial

A human-centered, editorial retirement planning website designed to demystify wealth management. Moving away from cold corporate fintech aesthetics, Five Pathways uses warm parchment tones, editorial typography, reassuring road metaphors, and a 5-pillar pathway structure to make retirement feel accessible, personal, and empowering.
